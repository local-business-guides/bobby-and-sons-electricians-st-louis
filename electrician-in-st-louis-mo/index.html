What to have ready before the call

A little preparation can make the conversation easier. You don’t need to diagnose the issue or take anything apart. Gather a few basic details instead:

  • What stopped working or changed?
  • When did you first notice it, and has it happened again?
  • Did a breaker switch off, or did you notice a smell, sound, or visible damage?
  • Is anyone else in the home seeing the same problem?
